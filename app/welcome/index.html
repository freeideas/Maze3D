Monster Maze

Run the maze. Find the glowing square. Something down there is looking for you.

Play

How it works

  • Other players are in the maze with you. You can run right through each other.
  • The monster can't be passed. If it touches you, you start the level again and your clock goes back to zero.
  • It sees through walls, but it isn't clever. You are a little faster than it is.
  • Finish a level and you go up to the next, bigger one.
  • Wear headphones. You can hear where the monster is: left or right, and muffled when it is behind you.

Controls

Arrow keys or W, A, S, D, or swipe anywhere. Once you are inside the maze, up always means straight ahead. Left and right turn you that way and keep going, and down turns you around. Tap or press space to stop.
